# Round 59 review: accessibility and performance (area "a11y-perf")

Build: the shared wrangler dev server at http://127.0.0.1:4180 (four-species fixture corpus). Chromium 1194 through Playwright 1.63, Node 22. Scripts and screenshots: /tmp/review59/a11y/. Private pages were measured with a collection written straight into the vault's IndexedDB (`inject()` in lib.mjs, generated by gen.mjs from changes the UI itself wrote through seed.mjs): 2 places, 1 seed batch, and 4, 12, 300 or 3,000 plants with acquire and water events.

Limits: cultifolio.com is not reachable from here, so nothing was measured on the real corpus or the edge; photographs on iNaturalist, Commons and GBIF do not load here (I stubbed them where it mattered and say so). No real screen reader was used: semantics come from the accessibility tree (Playwright aria snapshots) and DOM measurement. A phone's on-screen keyboard cannot be emulated, so the focus handler with the keyboard open is untested. The machine ran at load 4 to 9 on 2 CPUs during the timing runs; throttled timings carry that noise and are reported as measurements, not failures, unless the cause is structural.

A measuring trap worth knowing for the next reviewer: a Playwright `fullPage` screenshot in Chromium drops touch emulation for the rest of the page's life (`(pointer: coarse)` false, `--tap` back to 40 px). My first tap-target pass was wrong for that reason; every tap figure below comes from a pass with no full-page screenshots.

## Findings

### 1. P2. Watering by keyboard drops focus to the page; the toast's Undo is far away and vanishes under the keyboard user's focus (confirmed)

- `/plants` (src/routes/plants/+page.svelte:221) and `/today` (src/routes/today/+page.svelte:269): the Water button is `disabled` while its write runs (`disabled={watering === a.id}`, `disabled={!!watering ...}`). Disabling the focused button sends focus to `<body>`. Measured: Tab to "Record 2026-0006 watered today", Enter; `document.activeElement` is `BODY` at +50, +200, +500 and +1500 ms. Same on Today after "Water 2 here" (the button is then replaced by "Watered ✓").
- The Undo is in the toast, which sits at the top of `<main>` (src/routes/+layout.svelte:250). From row 2026-0006 on a 12-plant list it took 23 Shift+Tabs to reach it.
- The toast hides after 8 s whatever has focus (src/lib/ui/toast.svelte.ts:13-19). With focus on its Undo button I waited 8.5 s: the toast was gone and focus was on `BODY` again. Ten toast-only Undos use this (plant page water, move, entry removed, plant removed; plants list water; place water, feed and move; batch failed and removed; lightbox photo removed).
- The live region is created with its text (`{#if toast.text}<div role="status" aria-live="polite">`, ToastBar.svelte:15-17). A MutationObserver saw "inserted live region with text: "2026-0005 watered. Undo"". Many screen readers announce only changes to a region that already exists.
- Should: focus stays on a control; Undo is reachable next; a toast with an action does not expire while it has focus or hover; the region is always in the DOM.
- Smallest fix: use `aria-disabled` plus a guard in `water()` instead of `disabled` (or focus the row's "watered today" text after the write). Keep the toast's `role="status"` element mounted and swap only its text. In `show()`, pause the timer on `focusin`/`pointerenter` of the toast and resume on leave. Today's own inline "Watered just now · Undo" is the right pattern: reached in under 6 Tabs, and it restored the page text exactly.

### 2. P2. Layout shift of 0.20 to 0.29 on every warm load of the grower's lists (confirmed)

- This is the same with the service worker in control, without throttling, and with web fonts blocked, so it is not this machine. Measured CLS on warm loads: `/plants` 0.266 (12 plants) and 0.287 (300); `/today` 0.199; `/places` 0.035 to 0.150; `/propagation` 0.078 to 0.137. Throttled cold loads are the same (0.33 on `/plants`).
- The source is always `FOOTER.credits` (layout-shift attribution "footer y 273->0 h 266->0"). While the collection opens, `main` holds only the head and "Opening your collection…", so the footer is drawn in mid-screen. The list then pushes it out.
- Should: under 0.1.
- Smallest fix: on private routes, give `main` a `min-height: 100dvh`, or leave the footer out until `collection.ready` (src/routes/+layout.svelte:260).

### 3. P2. Section 4.1 claim "focus never under a bar" fails for date fields (confirmed)

- `/propagation/new` at 390, Tab to the end and then Shift+Tab back. From "How many seeds" into "Sown" (`#s-date`), the field stops at y=19 to 66 under the 49 px top bar. Its centre hits the crumb link (screenshot shots/sdate-14.png). The tab walk logged this for all four of the date field's internal stops.
- Cause: `keepFocusClear` returns unless `el.matches(':focus-visible')` (src/lib/ui/focus.ts:89). At the handler's frame, a date input entered by Shift+Tab answered `false` (logged `['s-date', false, 465, true]`, while text fields answered `true`). Ten date inputs use this path (plant form, plant page, batch form, batch page, lightbox).
- Should: the date field is scrolled clear like a text field.
- Smallest fix: treat `input[type=date|time|month|datetime-local]` as keyboard focus. Better, record the last input modality (keydown versus pointerdown) and use that instead of `:focus-visible`.
- Otherwise the claim holds. Tab and Shift+Tab through all 32 routes at 390 and 1280 (reduced motion), and again with smooth scrolling on five pages, found no other control with its centre covered. The plants list's chip row at 390 was clean.

### 4. P2. Forced colours: the selected state of every toggle, and the focused field on the two add forms, cannot be seen (confirmed)

- With `forcedColors: 'active'`, the pressed and unpressed chips on `/plants` compute to the same thing: "Growing pressed=true bg=rgb(255,255,255) border=rgb(0,0,0) 1px" and "Due pressed=false bg=rgb(255,255,255) border=rgb(0,0,0) 1px". Screenshots shots/fc_plants.png and shots/fc__by_genus.png show "Your species / All 4", "Genus / Origin / Family", the climate chips and the top nav's current page all looking unselected.
- Cause: the state is a background fill only (`.chipbtn.on`, `.seg > .on`, `nav.tabs a.on`: src/lib/ui/theme.css:205, 257, 262). Nothing in src has a `forced-colors` rule.
- Focused fields on `/plants/new` and `/propagation/new`: `.field input:focus { outline: 0; border-color: var(--accent); box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px var(--accent-soft) }` (plants/new/+page.svelte:265, propagation/new/+page.svelte:279). This beats the theme's 2 px `input:focus-visible` outline. In forced colours the shadow is removed and the border goes from rgb(0,0,0) to rgba(5,0,73,0.8), so only the caret marks focus (shots/fieldfocus-active.png). In normal colours the ring is a 1 px border change plus a 3 px rgb(226,241,235) halo, about 1.1:1 against the white card.
- Smallest fix: add `@media (forced-colors: active) { .on, [aria-pressed=true], [aria-current=page] { forced-color-adjust: none; background: Highlight; color: HighlightText; } }`. Delete the two pages' `outline: 0` so the theme's outline applies.
- Sound here: every other focus stop on eight pages kept an outline in forced colours, and the charts stay legible.

### 5. P2. Larger text: the phone tab bar loses "Today", and nine pages scroll sideways at 320 px (confirmed)

- I set the browser's text size in the profile (`webkit.webprefs.default_font_size`), not a CSS override.
- The tab bar (`#tabbar`, `grid-template-columns: repeat(5, 1fr)`, src/routes/+layout.svelte:331) is fixed, so its overflow cannot be scrolled to. Measured right edge of "Today":
  - At 390 px: 390 at 100%, 390 at 125%, 390 at 150%, 401 at 175%, 458 at 200%.
  - At 320 px: 344 at 150%, 458 at 200%.
  - At 200% the bar shows "Species, My plants, Places, Propag" (shots/crop-z_today.png); the "Today" tab cannot be reached from the bar. The menu still has it.
- Fix: `repeat(5, minmax(0, 1fr))` with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, or icons only once the labels do not fit.
- Horizontal scroll at 320 px with 200% text:
  - `/plants/2026-0001` 391>320 (`span.pill.w "not watered for 30 d"` does not wrap, and the h1 breaks as "Copia / poa / humili / s").
  - All four species pages, 347 to 355 (`div.acts` "Add one to my plants" and the glance cards).
  - `/about/how` and `/privacy` 367 (the unbroken "github.com/zomethingje-eng/cultifolio" link).
  - `/propagation` 340 (the batch `li`).
  - `/propagation/S2026-001` 362 (the count cards).
  - `/?by=family` 323 ("Welwitschiaceae").
- At 390 px with 200% text only the plant page scrolls, by 1 px (391>390), so the round's claim (it names `/about/formats`, `/propagation` and compare's picker) holds at 390 but not at 320. At 320 px with 100% text (WCAG reflow), nothing scrolls on any route.
- Fixes: `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the pill, the repository link and the cards; `min-width: 0` on the card grids; `flex-wrap` on `.acts`.

- Species page section row (Cultivation, Climate, Habitat, Photographs, Related, Registers): 40 tall.
- Species page source links (GBIF, Wikidata, Wikipedia, iNaturalist, POWO): 31 tall.
- "You grow" plant numbers: 75x25.

- Catalogue letter index C/R/W: 30x30.
- Compare: "Remove" is 50x32 and the species name links are 36 tall.
- Plant page: setup rows ("Add a photograph", "Measure it") 36 tall; `a.tap` "Set its light", "Set its floor", "The full cultivation sheet" 37 to 38 (the class is called `tap`); entry "×" 40x40; More menu items 39 tall; "···" is 41 wide.

### 7. P2. Private routes are blank until their JavaScript runs: 4.5 s first paint on a throttled phone (measured)

- `ssr = false` on `/plants`, `/places`, `/today`, `/propagation`, `/labels`, `/sync` and `/backup`. The HTML is 50 KB (13 KB gzip) of inline CSS and script with no text.
- Slow 4G plus 4x CPU, cold cache, no service worker, 300 plants:
  - `/plants`: FCP 4,504 to 4,608 ms, list ready 6,302 to 6,870 ms (3 runs).
  - Server-rendered `/`: FCP 1,112 to 1,272 ms.
- Return visits are served by the service worker, so this costs the first visit and any visit after the cache is gone.
- Fix: render the layout shell (top bar, tab bar, page head, "Opening your collection…") on the server for these routes. The page knows nothing private until the vault opens anyway.

### 8. P2. Opening a large collection: the long-tail path reads each change with its own `get`, and is slower than folding from scratch (measured)

Time to "N growing" on `/plants`:

| Collection | Load | CPU 1x | CPU 4x |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300 plants, 7,241 changes | first (full fold) | 696 ms | 2,232 ms |
| 300 plants | later (snapshot) | 253 to 437 ms | 1,434 to 1,479 ms |
| 3,000 plants, 72,041 changes | first (full fold, e.g. after the FOLD_RULES 4 deploy) | 4,086 ms | 10,401 ms |
| 3,000 plants | later | 387 to 489 ms | 1,319 to 1,793 ms |
| 3,000 plants | snapshot older than 72,041 arrivals | 8,552 ms | 17,649 ms |

- At 3,000 plants and 4x CPU, `/today` is ready in 3.7 to 4.1 s and the front page in 3.0 s.
- The last row of the table happens when a snapshot is older than the log: a large pull or merge in a tab that closed before `saveFold`.
- CPU profile of that load: 1,943 ms in native `get`, 289 ms in `get result`, and 311 ms in idb's add and remove of event listeners. These come from `arrivalsAfter` (src/lib/db/vault.ts:725-734): `Promise.all(rows.map((r) => ch.get(r.t)))`, one request per change. `changesByKeys` (vault.ts:505) does the same.
- Fix: past a few hundred rows, read `changes` with one `getAll()` and pick from it. Or treat a tail over FOLD_REFRESH as a rebuild, which costs half as much.

### 9. P3. Species page maps: 118 KB gzip on every first species visit, revalidated on every view (measured)

- `/maps/land50.svg` is 254 KB (92 KB gzip) and `/maps/land110.svg` is 66 KB (26 KB gzip), used by src/lib/map/still.ts:31 and :90. Together they are the largest download on a species page, ahead of the species route's own JS (27 KB gzip).
- Both are served `cache-control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`, so a visitor without the service worker revalidates both on every species page.
- Fix: put them under `_app/immutable` with a content hash, or crop and simplify the 50 m land per region.

### 10. P3. Front page at 701 px or wider with a collection autofocuses the search box (confirmed)

- At 1280 with plants, `document.activeElement` after load is `INPUT.searchbar`, so the first Tab goes to "Your species", past the skip link and the top bar. Shift+Tab returns. A screen reader starts in a field before the h1.
- Source: src/routes/+page.svelte:55 (`focusOnDesktop`).
- Every other route, both widths, with and without a collection: the first Tab reaches "Skip to content". So the claim in section 4.5 holds for directly loaded private pages.
- Fix: drop the autofocus, or offer "/" as a shortcut to the search.

### 11. P3. Compare is a grid of divs, and its picker is silent (confirmed from the accessibility tree)

- The aria snapshot of `.cmp` reads as one run of text: "Coldest night (a floor where one is read) Climate pending 6.5 °C 1st-percentile night …". Nothing ties a value to its species column (src/routes/compare/+page.svelte:130-145).
- Typing in `#cmp-q` shows result links with no status message and no arrow-key movement; ArrowDown left focus in the field.
- Fix: give rows and cells `role="row"`, `role="rowheader"` and `role="cell"` (or use a real table), and add a `role="status"` line "N matches".

### 12. P3. Smaller semantics (confirmed)

- The plant and batch h1 names have no space between number and name: aria snapshot `heading "2026-0001Copiapoa humilis" [level=1]`, and likewise "S2026-001Ariocarpus fissuratus". Fix: add a space or a visually hidden separator.
- `<div class="today" aria-label="Today">` (src/lib/ui/Today.svelte:168): axe flags `aria-prohibited-attr` (needs review). A name on a generic div is ignored. Fix: give it `role="region"` or drop the label.
- The Add button on `/plants/new` carries `aria-live="polite"` (plants/new/+page.svelte:252), so every relabel ("Checking the name…", "Adding…") is announced from a button. A separate status line would be cleaner.
- Every species photograph has `alt=""` (6 on Copiapoa cinerea). The strip link is named, so this is a choice, but the hero photograph is content.
- `/labels`: the preview text is 10 px (numbers) and 8.3 px (care line). It is a true-size print preview, but it is the only text under 11 px anywhere. QR SVGs have no role or name.

### 13. P3. Font swap shifts layout where the fallback metrics do not apply (measured, environment-dependent)

- On this Linux machine Georgia maps to DejaVu Serif and Arial to Liberation Sans, so the metric-matched fallbacks (theme.css:11-17) do not apply.
- Throttled CLS with web fonts: front page 0.154, species page 0.151 at 390. The action row wraps "h 88->44" when Public Sans arrives. With fonts blocked and photographs stubbed: 0.000 and 0.002.
- DM Mono, used for every number pill, has no fallback override at all.
- On Android (Roboto), iOS and Windows this is probably absorbed; on desktop Linux it is not.

### 14. P3. Species page hydration and the rain formatter (measured)

- At 4x CPU the species page's hydration long task is 400 to 930 ms. Throttled TBT is 0.8 to 1.6 s on the species page and 0.6 to 0.9 s on the front page, under load.
- The largest single app function by self time is the rain formatter `d` in chunk TMKZlN6M (177 ms at 4x). It calls `Math.round(e).toLocaleString('en-US')` once per value, which builds a number formatter each call. A module-level `new Intl.NumberFormat('en-US')` removes most of that.
- The species route chunk (nodes/28, 84 KB raw) also carries the share card's canvas code. A dynamic `import()` on "Share card" would keep it off first load.

### 15. P3. Service worker install fetches 825 KB (measured)

- 134 entries: 2,122 KB decoded, 825 KB gzip (JS 304, fonts 178, HTML shells 129, SVG maps 118, PNG 61, CSS 33).
- The 12 HTML shells are 48 to 69 KB raw each, because every shell inlines about 50 KB of CSS that is also precached as 32 CSS files.
- That is a lot for a first visit on a metered phone. Leaving land50.svg and the italic font out of the precache would cut about 155 KB.

## Ranked fix list

1. Keep focus on Water, mount the toast region once, and pause the toast while focused (finding 1).
2. Reserve `main`'s height, or hide the footer, until the collection is open on private routes (finding 2; CLS 0.27 to 0.29 to under 0.05).
3. Add forced-colours rules for `.on`, `[aria-pressed=true]` and `[aria-current]`, and delete the two add forms' `outline: 0` (finding 4).
4. In focus.ts, treat date inputs (or keyboard modality) as focus-visible (finding 3).
5. Tab bar `minmax(0, 1fr)` and wrapping; `overflow-wrap` on the plant pill, the repository link and the cards (finding 5).
6. Today tick box inside a 44 px label; species section row and source links to `--tap` (finding 6).
7. Server-render the private shell (finding 7; FCP about 4.5 s to about 1.2 s cold).
8. `arrivalsAfter` and `changesByKeys` with one `getAll` (finding 8; the 3,000-plant tail load 8.6 s to under the full fold's 4.1 s).
9. Hash and immutable-cache the land maps, or regional crops (finding 9; 118 KB per species visit).
10. Cache `Intl.NumberFormat` and lazy-load the share card (finding 14).
11. The P3 semantics items (findings 10 to 13, 15).

## Checked and sound

- axe-core: 0 violations on 128 runs (32 routes, all four fixture species, catalogue groupings, both 404s, offline, compare empty and full, both about pages, privacy, and every private page with a collection; light and dark; 390 and 1280).
- Field edges at rest (section 4.2): text, search, select, date, number and textarea fields measure 3.28 to 4.91:1 in light and 4.59 to 7.62:1 in dark, including opened forms (Log, Edit, place edit, new place, settings). A focused field's border reverts to the page's own, but a 2 px outline is drawn, except on the two add forms (finding 4).
- No text under 11 px anywhere except the label preview, with SVG text scaled by its transform. Chart text is 5.4 to 8.9:1 in both themes.
- Links in text are underlined: no inline link in p, li, dd, td or figcaption without an underline, in either theme.
- Skip link: first Tab on every private page loaded directly, at 390 and 1280.
- Lightbox, keyboard only: opens from "Open photograph: …" and focuses Close; `aria-modal` with the page behind inert; ArrowRight changes the live text to "…, 2 of 3"; Escape closes and returns focus to the opener. Same at 390.
- Species picker combobox (with the name service refused, as here): `role=combobox`, `aria-expanded=true`, `aria-activedescendant` follows ArrowDown and ArrowUp, the option has `aria-selected`, and Escape closes. Adding a plant by keyboard alone works, and the new page focuses `main`.
- Place form refusal: `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` on the field, focus returns to it, and "Not saved: abc is not a temperature…" is in the status line.
- Compare by keyboard: "Compare" toggles `aria-pressed`, the tray link is reachable, and adding and removing species works.
- Landmarks and headings: one h1 per page, no skipped levels, banner, named navs (Breadcrumb, Main, Tabs, Sections, Group by), and named regions for scrolling tables.
- Charts: the climograph is `role=img`, named, with `aria-describedby` to its description; the maps are named.
- Reduced motion: no animation or transition on any element of the six pages checked (front, species, plants, Today, plant, compare), `scroll-behavior: auto`, and the toast does not animate.
- WCAG reflow: 320 px at 100% text, no horizontal scroll on any route.
- Memory: 80 client-side navigations over 10 routes with 300 plants. After forced GC the heap goes 7.0 to 9.2 MB in the first 20 and 9.7 MB after 80; DOM nodes and listeners are flat after the first 20. With 3,000 plants the live heap is 23.5 MB.
- The tap-token list the round names: Undo 56x44, Water 64x44, sort 44, card actions 44 tall, reference-photo offer 44.
