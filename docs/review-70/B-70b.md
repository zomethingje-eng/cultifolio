## 1. `--fill inat-ids`: R47's rule, measured on all 42

**Rule** (`judgeTaxon` / `judgeIdTaxon` in `sources/inat.ts`; used by the build and by both fills):
- **Own names.** A name is one of the species' own when the genus and `spellingKey(epithet)` agree (`isOwnName`). Such a name is taken without asking anything.
- **The autonym.** The autonym of an own name is the species (Tricyrtis formosana formosana). It is then given up for iNaturalist's own taxon of the species, found by name (452309).
- **Any other name** is matched to GBIF's backbone, `match?verbose=true`:
  - **Taken** when GBIF holds it as this species: EXACT or FUZZY, as itself or as a synonym, or among the alternatives.
  - **Refused** when GBIF holds it EXACT as another species of this reference.
  - **Refused** when GBIF reads it two ways, one of them another species here.
- **Departure from R47: Kew.** When the backbone does not place the name in this reference, Kew decides next (GBIF's copy of WCVP, one or two requests):
  - Kew holds it as a synonym of one of the species' own names: **taken**.
  - Kew holds it as a synonym of another species here: **refused**.
  - Kew holds this species as a synonym of it: **taken**.
- **Last, the epithet.** The taxon is taken only when its epithet is this species' (`spellingKey`, or a personal-name stem: selloa and selloana), and no other species of the family here has that epithet.

**Why Kew.** Without it, R47's rule refuses three of your 35 right follows, all the same plant:
- Ranunculus ficaria → Ficaria verna. GBIF holds Ficaria verna as an accepted species outside the reference; the epithet differs.
- Callistemon lanceolatus → Melaleuca citrina. The epithet differs.
- Hibiscus sabdariffa → Sabdariffa gossypiifolia. The epithet differs.

Kew settles each one:
- Ficaria verna is Kew's synonym of Ranunculus ficaria L.
- Kew holds Callistemon lanceolatus as a synonym of Melaleuca citrina.
- Kew holds Hibiscus sabdariffa as a synonym of Sabdariffa gossypiifolia.

Kew also refuses a wrong follow. It holds Echinofossulocactus ochoterenanus as a synonym of Stenocactus ochoterenanus, another species here, so Stenocactus boedekerianus does not take it.

## 2. Photographs lost in the rebuild

This was 116 "photograph sets from the files". **Fix:** the species' keys in `bulk/keys.json` (`BulkSources.covered`) are the download's; a species outside them goes to the API. A species built under a name the download did not use is now loaded by its key.

Anisocampium niponicum shows it: of its 949 photographed records, 177 are open, so 40 drawn blind give about 8 open ones, exactly the rebuild's 8. **Fix:** open records are drawn first, as the API asks for them, and NC records only fill up to 40.
- **Which species had which cause** depends on your `keys.json`, which I do not have. Anisocampium fits the second cause exactly; the drops to none fit the first.
- **Ranunculus ficaria** lost 61 iNaturalist photographs to the follow refusal. The id is taken back now.

## 5. `--fill wikidata`

- **Waiting.** Each request is now asked in rounds: `mwGet`'s five asks, then a pause of 60, 120 or 300 s. There are up to `LAG_SPELL` rounds, about 40 minutes in all, before it gives up.
- **Resuming.** A run that stops keeps what it read in `fill-wikidata-progress.json`: the items carrying their own key, and the changes it found. The next run passes them by, and the file is deleted when a run ends.
- **rebuild-wd.txt** is written only by a run that ends.
- **`--keys` with a missing file** now says "rebuild-wd.txt is not there, which --fill wikidata writes when it runs to the end … Nothing was built."
- **Tests:** `r70b2-wikidata-fill.test.ts`.
