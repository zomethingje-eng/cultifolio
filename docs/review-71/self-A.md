Every number below is over all 8,947 dossiers of the live corpus (`/home/claude/r72data/s/v2`, corpus 4c784c5dc7ca2448) against Kew's `wcvp_names.csv` in this workspace, unless it says otherwise. ## Findings

**1. 227 pages cite an author who is not the author of the name they link; Ixora coccinea is one of them (confirmed).**
`a1b_ipni_author.py` reads Kew's row for each page's own IPNI number: on 227 pages that row is the page's words by another author, and on 250 more it is the same publishing author cited otherwise ("S.Mc Cabe" for S.W.McCabe, "Steyerm. & L.B.Smith"). Among the 227: Ixora coccinea "Comm. ex Lam." (an invalid name Kew files under Embothrium coccineum) beside IPNI 753844-1, Ixora coccinea L.; Fragaria virginiana "Duchesne" beside Miller's 318974-2; Diospyros kaki "L.f." beside Thunb.; Durio zibethinus "Murray" beside L.; Spathodea campanulata "Beauverd" (another botanist) beside P.Beauv.; Coryphantha echinus "(Engelm.) Britton & Rose" beside Orcutt's; Tradescantia zebrina "Bosse" beside Regel. Twelve show a backbone citation cut off after its parenthesis ("Mesembryanthemum pallens (Sol.)", "Colchicum burchellii (Baker)", Albuca somersetianum and Yucca luminosa with no author at all), and five carry a year ("Hoodia gordonii Sweet, 1830"). The full list, classified, is `a1_classified.tsv` and `a1b_ipni_author.txt`.

Root cause. `build.ts` line 268 shows the backbone's author unless `kewName` (line 682) returns one, and `kewName` runs only for an orphan key (line 249: no GBIF synonym at all) and then only when GBIF holds the words by Kew's author as exactly one other usage (line 696). Ixora coccinea has one synonym, "Ixora coccinea Comm.", so it is never asked; and GBIF's usage "Ixora coccinea L." is DOUBTFUL with 2,651 records of its own. Round 71 wrote this as a guard test (`r71b-homonym-key.test.ts`: "a key with synonyms of its own is no orphan, and keeps the backbone's author (guard: Diospyros kaki L.f.)"). Meanwhile `--fill kew-links` (round 71) changed the links to Kew's number for the name, so the page now contradicts itself: the heading says one name, the IPNI and POWO links open another.

Fix (in the diff). The page cites the name it links. `kewIpni` names each number it gives (`labels`); the build cites Kew's row for the linked number whenever it is the page's own words, and records "GBIF's key K is X A; the page links Kew's N, X B, and the name is cited as Kew cites it", which `kewCitedNote` now shows under the name as it does for a homonym key. For the live corpus, an offline `--fill kew-author --bulk bulk` (`kewCitation`, `fillKewAuthor`) does the same from Kew's file. Dry run on a copy of the live corpus (`a1_fill_dryrun.log`): 683 pages change, 479 of substance (`a1_fill_changes.txt`) and 204 in spelling or punctuation only; 50 seconds, no requests. Borrowing names stays the orphan rule's. `/about/how` still describes only the orphan case and needs F's sentence: "the name is cited as Kew's entry for the number the page links".

**2. 275 pages list, as older names of the species, 379 names Kew accepts as species of their own, and say so only for six (confirmed).**
`a8_synonyms.py`: "Allium porrum L." on Allium ampeloprasum, "Vaccinium ashei Reade" and three more on Vaccinium corymbosum, five accepted Artocarpus on Artocarpus altilis, "Yucca jaegeriana" on Yucca brevifolia. Each is GBIF's synonym, so sourced, but `+page.server.ts` line 176 (`namedElsewhere`) checks only the reference's own species. A visitor reads "Also known as Allium porrum" with nothing to say Kew disagrees. Smallest fix: an offline fill (like the one above) that marks each stored synonym Kew accepts by the same author as a species, and the page lists those apart ("Kew accepts as a species of its own"). Not in the diff.

**3. 138 pages Kew holds by name and author link neither POWO nor IPNI (confirmed).**
`a2_ids.txt`, "no-powo-but-kew-has-one": mostly recent species with no Wikidata item or an item without numbers (Echeveria purpusiorum, Pelecyphora macromeris, Colchicum burchellii). The build asks Kew only when the item carries a number (`build.ts` line 450) and `fillKewLinks` skips a dossier with none (`corpus-fills.ts` line 150). Fix in the diff: the build links Kew's number when Wikidata answered with no number or no item, and the fill reads every dossier. A full `--fill kew-links` asks GBIF once per dossier (about 8,950 requests); the 153 without numbers alone would be 153.

**4. Five records say "Kew holds X only under another author (…), which is another name" of the same name (confirmed).**
`a1c_another_name.txt`: Monsonia mossamedensis Welw. (Kew: "Welw. ex Oliv."), Echeveria peacockii Croucher ("Croucher ex T.Moore & Mast."), Hibiscus acetosella Welw. ex Ficalho ("Welw. ex Hiern"), Albuca longifolia Fisch. ex Steud. ("Fisch. ex F.Dietr.") and Cochemiea matehualensis (finding 5). The sentence comes from `bulk.ts` line 649, which calls every row `ownByAuthor` did not take "another name", while `plainlyAnother` (the identifiers' rule) says these share their manuscript author and are not. Fix in the diff: "another name" only where `plainlyAnother`; otherwise "Kew cites X as X A, which shares the backbone's author only before or after "ex", and its entry is not read for this species". The range decision is unchanged.

**5. Cochemiea matehualensis lost a right POWO link (confirmed); Ceropegia macrocarpa lost a wrong one (confirmed).**
Cochemiea matehualensis: GBIF's author is "(Backeb. ex Spine)", cut off; Kew's only row of the words is "(Backeb.) M.H.J.van der Meer", the same combination of Neolloydia matehualensis Backeb. (which the page lists and takes its range from). `parentheticalAuthor` reads the basionym's author after "ex" ("Spine") and Kew's is "Backeb.", so no reading of `ownByAuthor` takes the row, the link 77320739-1 was dropped and the record calls van der Meer's name "another name". Fix in the diff: a fifth reading, the basionym's author either side of an "ex" when one citation has an ex-author in its parentheses and the other not. Over the whole corpus it changes the rows taken for two species only (`a_reading5_effect.txt`): Cochemiea matehualensis, and Gymnocalycium hossei, whose link already was Kew's. The page should link 77320739-1 again.
Ceropegia macrocarpa (Sprenger) Bruyns: Kew holds no row of Bruyns's combination; its only "Ceropegia macrocarpa" is J.N.Haage & E.Schmidt's unplaced homonym, so dropping 77238679-1 was right and the record's sentence is true. Kew holds the basionym, "Huernia macrocarpa E.Dammann & Sprenger" (77215887-1), and the page's own older name Huernia penzigii, both as synonyms of Huernia macrocarpa (A.Rich.) Schweinf. ex K.Schum. (98787-1). I would say on the page: "Kew does not hold this combination; it holds its basionym under Huernia macrocarpa", and leave POWO unlinked. The range could come from there, but GBIF's synonym list lacks the basionym, so `viaOlderNames` cannot reach it.

**6. Echinocereus marksianus links IPNI to a number IPNI does not have (confirmed, 1 page).**

## The open issues in my area

**Ixora coccinea's author (open 2).** Finding 1: the orphan rule never asks it. The diff cites Kew's L. on the next build, or at once with `--fill kew-author`.

**IPNI and POWO (594 disagreements).** `a2_ipni_powo_inputs.txt`: of the 582 lines in `ipni-powo.txt`, 574 now agree on Kew's number; 563 of them had an item IPNI that Kew's file does not hold (IPNI duplicates), seven an IPNI duplicate of the same words, and four another name (Origanum majorana's was Majorana hortensis, Drosera binata's Drosera dichotoma). The eight still disagreeing are the spellings of finding 9. Over the corpus, every POWO link Kew holds is Kew's for the name and author except six pages (`a2_ids.txt`, "powo-is-another-row"), and those six link the plant Kew accepts while citing a name Kew holds otherwise (finding 1).

**The lost POWO links (open 3).** Finding 5: Cochemiea's drop was wrong, Ceropegia macrocarpa's right.

**The ten Wikidata changes (open 1).** Read from Wikidata's `Special:EntityData`, one a second (`wdget.py`, answers in `wd/`). Each proposed item's POWO number is Kew's for the page's name and author: Raphionacme linearis K.Schum. (100771-1), Peperomia monticola Miq. (679159-1), Tulipa clusiana Redouté (542880-1), Othonna rosea Harv. (236135-1), Convolvulus cneorum L. (266046-1), Gunnera manicata Linden ex André (324610-2), Salvia azurea Michx. ex Vahl (455727-1), Agave ghiesbreghtii Verschaff. (30187195-2). Rubus parviflorus already has Q2614003 in the live corpus. Agave variegata: Q15523250 is the item for Manfreda variegata (Jacobi) Rose (label, taxon name and POWO 152327-2, which is the Manfreda name), and names Q391952 as its basionym; Q391952 is the item for the name Agave variegata, POWO 62333-1 = Agave variegata Jacobi, which Kew holds as a synonym of Manfreda variegata. Worth doing only if the one-command corpus step (process change 2) orders those fills anyway.

**Two pages for one plant (open 8).** Finding 7: seven pairs. **spellingKey (open 9).** `a5_spelling.ts` over all 365,813 species Kew accepts (hybrids included): seven pairs of distinct epithets joined, in seven genera. Two are Kew's own duplicate rows of one name and author in two genders (Epidendrum itacolomiensis/-e, Hieracium bectauatensis/-e), so the join is right. Five join two plants: Anthurium lancea Sodiro and Anthurium ×lanceum Engl. None of these genera holds the other spelling in the reference, and the only pair of reference pages the key joins is Conophytum calculus with "Conophytum calculus vanzylii", a page whose scientific name is a subspecies written without its rank. Against the reference's older names (`a5b_older.txt`) the 39 joins are Kew's own spellings of the page's name (the Cyphostemma -ense names, Clematis armandi) or of a GBIF older name. I would close the issue with a test that pins the seven, not change the key.

**Summaries.** `a4_summaries.py`: 4,474 summaries, 270 under another title, no article on two pages. Every other title is the name spelt otherwise, an older name GBIF lists, the item's English article, a product, or a genus Kew holds one species in. Checked against Kew: no title is a binomial Kew resolves to another plant (Rosa pimpinellifolia L. is Kew's synonym of Rosa spinosissima var. spinosissima); 23 titles that are GBIF older names carry no proof in the record, which the rule allows (`proofByName`). Monotypic-genus articles sometimes describe the genus ("Jeffersonia is a small genus", Kew holds one species in it); by the rule, not a fault.
