Round eighty (R): the 912 species without a GBIF key that read no key under scripts/extra-keys-kew.txt, and why.
Made 2026-10-21 by scripts/dev/scale/r80r-why-unkeyed.ts (the rule traced) and scripts/kew-extra-keys.ts (noKeyFor),
from scripts/species-list.txt, Kew's wcvp_names.csv of 2026-06-04 and GBIF's backbone export (round 75's simple.txt.gz).

By noKeyFor (scripts/extra-keys-kew-none.txt), 912 pages:
  lumped        647  GBIF files a name the rule would read as this plant under another page of the reference
                     (638 of them the plant's own name or one of its type; 9 only names of another type)
                     of the 647: 18 under a page with no Kew id (11 of those are the same plant twice: below)
  none          211  the backbone holds no usage of any of Kew's names for the plant
  other-type     45  only heterotypic names or taxa below (39), with pro parte synonyms (5) or a name read to two plants (1)
  other-author    8  the words only by another author (6 of the 8 are filed by GBIF under another page anyway)
  other           1  an autonym GBIF does not place (Dinteranthus wilmotianus subsp. wilmotianus)

No class has an unambiguous route to records under the rule as decided:
  - lumped: the records are already on the other page; giving them here too would put one record on two pages.
  - none: there is no key; a name match is refused (rule 3).
  - other-type, pro parte: refused by the owner's decision of round 73 and by GBIF's own word.
  - other-author: only by an author equivalence Kew's file does not state; and 6 of 8 are lumped as well.

The option not taken (an owner's decision): read a lumped page's own-type names' records here and leave them out of
the other page (record by record, by the record's own taxon key, as `except` does). GBIF's count API, the download's
filter, one request a second, 2026-10-21 (scripts/dev/scale/r79m-kew-key-counts.mjs on the 1,565 own-type keys):

  records: 958,969 (union), of which 287,988 human observations with images
  download growth: none. GBIF's taxonKey filter on the other page's key already holds them
    (Echinocereus viridiflorus 3084127: 6,898; with E. canus's 11071043, 13 records, asked together: 6,898)
