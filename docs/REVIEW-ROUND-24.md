# Review round twenty-four: two reviews of `2c9f46a`, triaged

The second reviewer's fifteenth pass (two findings, with fresh Lighthouse and axe runs: 93–100 on every page and view, zero A/AA violations) and the first reviewer's round twenty-three, whose walk was the one no round had made: two devices synced for six simulated weeks with offline stretches, a second tab, an audit after a hundred days, a backup and restore mid-sync, a stop and a rejoin. Everything agreed across devices every time. What did not hold is mostly about time: the same text edited on two devices, a page left open past midnight, and what counts as having seen a plant. Every finding was checked in the source or reproduced. All are real and all are done; the two reviewers' design notes that were not taken are at the end. "R1-n" is the second reviewer's; "R2-n" the first reviewer's.

## The log

1. **Free text edited on two devices offline kept one text and said nothing** (R2-1; the reviewer's one thing to fix). Field-level last-writer-wins is right for every field but a paragraph of observations. Now the device whose text lost writes what it said on the plant's log: a pulled change to `notes` (a plant's or a batch's) that wins over a non-empty text this device wrote itself, from another device, puts a line on the log, "Notes replaced by an edit made on another device; here they read: …". The line is written once, by the device that held the text; the other device logs nothing, since its text won; a tab of the same device is not another device. The same in the plant page's notes editor: a text that changed underneath an open editor (another tab, a pull) is logged before it is written over. Unit test: the winning pull logs the old text once; a losing change and the same device's other tab log nothing.
2. **A page left open overnight dated the morning's work yesterday** (R2-2). The plant page's log form, the place page's Water all and audit, and the batch page's four forms took their date when the page loaded. The log form now takes today when it opens; the place page reads the day when the action runs; the batch page's always-open forms follow the calendar through a small store that checks the day when the tab is shown or focused and once a minute, and only a date the grower has not typed over follows it.
3. **"Last seen" counted things nobody saw, and a same-day miss lost to a same-day watering** (R1-1, R2-3). The date-only comparison from round twenty-three let any action dated the audit's day cancel the miss, whichever came first; automatic lines (a place removed, a rename, the number repair) and Water all made every plant "seen today"; a future-dated event put the sighting in the future. Now the log is read in its own order (date, then id, newest first): a watering at 09:00 and a miss at 17:00 are two lines and the later one is the answer, and a tick after a miss clears it. Events carry `auto: true` when the app wrote them or a place-wide action made them (Water all, Feed all, a place removal's Move lines, a rename's note, a notes-replaced line), and such lines never count as a sighting; nor does the number repair's note, nor an event dated after today. The formats page lists the field. Unit tests: water then miss, miss then tick, and the automatic, bulk and future cases.
4. **Removing a place could leave a plant at an empty link, or log a move for a dead plant** (R2-4). A plant filed under a place that another device had already removed was matched by raw id and missed; it is now found through `placeOf`, so it moves up too. Dead and archived plants are refiled without a Move line and not counted in the toast. The plant page says "its place was removed; no place now" instead of linking to nothing when a place is gone from under it, and the setup step "Give it a place" fires on that too. Unit test.
5. **Two edit-form slips** (R2-5). Changing only "What it is" filled "name as received" with the unchanged name and logged nothing; it now fills that only when the full name changed, and logs "Now recorded as a cultivar". Saving any edit of a hybrid, even its price, dropped its key; a hybrid's key now goes only when its name changes, so a nothospecies picked from the list keeps the key the list gave it.

## The site's claims

6. **"Every address key is dropped within two days" was not quite true** (R2-6). The KV byte counter had a two-day TTL that every write renewed, so a key first written at 00:01 and last at 23:59 lived nearly three days; it now expires at a fixed moment, the midnight that ends the following day, which is also when the counter object's sweep drops its keys. `totals()` counted refunded addresses at zero; it counts only positive keys. `/about/how` says "within two days of the day it was written (at the UTC midnight that ends the following day)". Tests: the expiry passed to KV; a refunded address not among the addresses.
7. **The formats test still missed half the page's facts, and three checks could not fail** (R2-7). The reviewer's mutation run (215 single-fact edits, 105 uncaught) is the measure. Now the test reads the record interfaces out of `types.ts` and requires each kind's field list on the page to equal the interface's, `id` aside, so a field added to the code or dropped from the page fails; the provenance, status and name-kind enumerations are read from the type unions; the 507 body is read from `VaultFull`'s own response; the batch plaintext shape is the engine's `logBatch` and its name must pass `BATCH_NAME`, the pattern the Worker now validates with (moved to the shared module beside `batchName`); the stale comments calling the fingerprint a SHA-256 content hash are corrected. Changing `(growing, archived, dead)` to `(growing, sold, dead)` fails the test now.
8. **Three facts** (R2-8). `package.json` said MIT; it says AGPL-3.0-only, as the licence file and README do. The Show HN draft said tenth-degree cells of ~144 pixels; the grid is 0.05°, 36 of CHELSA's 30-arc-second pixels, as `/about/how` says. DEPLOY.md pointed at section 1 for a check that is in section 2.

## Living with it

9. **Offline read as an error, and "Synced" appeared before any run** (R2-9). A run that never reaches the server (a `TypeError` from fetch, or the browser's own offline flag) now sets the status to "Offline" with "21 changes kept here, sent when you are back online"; before the first run of a page's life the status is "Not checked yet" with the last sync's time and "checking now", and "Synced" appears only after a run has finished here. The header icon's title says the same.
10. **After Stop syncing, the page led with making a second vault** (R2-10). Stopping (and a replace from backup, which stops) now remembers which vault the device was in; the page says so ("This device was in vault abc123… until 2 min ago; its collection is still here"), leads with "Rejoin with your key", and offers "Set up a new, separate vault" second, saying what that would do. Making a new vault forgets the record. e2e: stop, the lead, the rejoin, and the offline status.
11. **Today never mentioned care** (R2-11). Two lines, from the log and not from a schedule, in the words the plants list and the place pages already use: "14 of 14 plants not watered, or not recorded as watered, for three weeks or more" (to the Due chip) and "3 plants missed at the last audit or not seen for ninety days" (to Places). e2e: a watering dated a month ago puts the line on Today.
12. **Counts that meant something other than they said** (R2-12). A place's "Last watered" card now says it is the most recently watered plant and how long the longest-waiting one has waited, and how many have never been watered. "Waiting to send" says it counts field changes ("a note is one; a new plant is several"). The restore preview's "species notes" were the app's own per-species records; they are "species records" with what they are in brackets, and the missing space before them is back. The pot-up notice ran its numbers together because Svelte trims a space at a block's edge; the separator is an expression now.
13. **Cuttings had seed words left, and a count of 0 read as no count** (R2-13). The batch edit form hides "Seed from", the lot and "Seed provenance" for a vegetative method and saves the batch as vegetative; the date check says "before the batch was started"; the count message says "the total struck so far"; a recorded count, 0 included, is a count on the fourth card.
14. **At Apia the frost line's weekday sat beside the previous day's date** (R2-14). Round twenty-three kept clock time there on purpose; what was missing was the table saying its days are solar. The column is headed "Night (by the sun)", with the rule in its tooltip.
15. **Three browser tests left the page before their own write was on screen** (R1-2, R2-15). Two clicked Add and navigated at once, which the round-twenty-three key check turned into a race on a slow machine; one added a place and left before its row appeared. Each now waits for what it depends on (the plant's URL, the place's row), no fixed delays. The Add button says "Checking the name…" while the check runs and "Adding…" while it files, since a button that waits silently looks stuck.
