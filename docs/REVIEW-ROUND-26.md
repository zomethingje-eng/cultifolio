# Review round twenty-six: two reviews of `4799e5d`, triaged

The first reviewer's round twenty-five, from three angles (an attempt to break `notesBase`, the watering figure and numbering by acquired year; a walk as a grower who sells and swaps; the formats test and the deploy tooling), and the second reviewer's seventeenth pass (one finding, the future acquisition date, with Lighthouse 97–100 everywhere and zero axe violations; they reproduced the 21-, 30- and 31-day watering cases on production and found them right). Every finding was checked in the source or reproduced. All are real. All but two parts are done; those two are named at the end with why. "R1-n" is the first reviewer's; "R2" the second reviewer's one finding.

## Records that came out wrong

1. **A price-only edit to a nothospecies wrote its own name into its parentage** (R1-1; the reviewer's one thing to fix). The edit form fell back to the typed name when a hybrid had an epithet and no stated parentage, so "× Graptoveria titubans" printed "Graptoveria titubans (Graptoveria titubans)" on its label. A nothospecies' parentage is what the grower states; the fallback is gone.
2. **A future acquisition date minted a permanent number in the wrong year** (R1-3, R2). Round twenty-five made the acquired date decide the number's year without bounding it, so `2099-01-01` typed for 2026 minted `2099-0001` for good, and nothing said the rule. The add form and the edit form refuse a date after today with the log form's sentence, before any check or write; the preview says "its year is the year acquired"; Settings › Numbering states the rule for plants and batches. e2e: the preview shows `2099-0001`, Add is refused, nothing is minted.
3. **The `notesBase` check had four gaps** (R1-2). The base was matched to any `notesBase` change for the record in the incoming list, so a restore carrying an older base made a knowing edit look blind; a save that re-sent unchanged notes recorded a base, after which the next real edit's base was dropped as unchanged and the edit went out blind; a push cut every two thousand changes, or halved for size, could separate a notes change from its base; and the batch page took its base when Save was pressed, not when the form opened. Now `put` emits the base exactly when the notes change and always then, stamped right after them by the same writer; a reader pairs each edit with the same writer's next base and no other notes change between; `cutBefore` moves a batch cut one step back rather than split the pair; the batch form takes its base when it opens. The conflict line no longer names the other device by hex. Unit tests: an unchanged re-send writes no base, a real edit always does; a restore with an old base of the other device's does not log a knowing edit; a cut never falls between the pair.
4. **A removed plant could not be brought back by merging a backup** (R1-4). The newer deletion wins the merge, so the only way back was Replace, which loses everything since. A removed plant's page now offers "Restore this plant", which revives the record with its log and photographs (they were never gone), and the removal text says so. e2e.
5. **Nothing a grower gets wrong could be undone** (R1-5). Toasts can carry an action. Water all and Feed all offer Undo for eight seconds and remove exactly the lines they wrote; removing a plant offers Undo, which restores it and returns to its page. e2e for both.
6. **A never-watered plant and the place card disagreed with Today** (R1-6). The plant page's due pill and Since-watered card required a watering; they now read `careDays`, saying "no watering recorded in N d" and counting from the day the record was made, as the list and Today do. The place card no longer says "every plant here was watered that day" when one never was, says "N with no watering recorded", and counts the due.
7. **After Stop syncing and then a Replace, the sync page still led with Rejoin** (R1-7). The former-vault record is rewritten as `replaced` whenever it exists, sync on or off.
8. **/plants sorted unplaced plants first and did not fold accents** (R1-8). Unplaced plants sort last; the search folds diacritics as the species search does.

## What the tools and pages claimed

9. **The live check passed a deploy with no corpus** (R1-9). With no `index.json` in R2 the Worker falls back to the four fixture species, the sitemap lists them, and every check answered. `/api/corpus` saying `fixture` now fails the check unless `LIVE_CHECK_FIXTURE_OK=1`, which only a local `wrangler dev` sets; DEPLOY.md says so.
10. **"Request windows are dropped as each ten minutes ends" was false in two ways** (R1-10). Windows were pruned only when a key was made, and only in that key's bucket. Every bucket's expired windows go whenever any key is made, each bucket judged by its own window length, with the address's own colons (IPv6) left alone.
11. **"Fails closed without the binding" was true only without the counter object** (R1-11). With the object bound and no KV, creation was allowed, on a deploy with no per-address byte allowance and memory-only rate limits. Creation without KV is refused (a 503, "not taking new vaults for now") whatever else is bound, and DEPLOY.md says why.
12. **The formats test: eight checks that could not fail, and the facts it missed** (R1-12). The refusal statuses live in one `STATUS` map the routes and the Worker use, and the page's figures are compared with it; a backup is built through the writer and its zip's entry names compared with the page's; `batchKey` and `photoKey` are compared with the page's object paths; the 507 body's word is the page's, not a literal; the eight dead checks are deleted. Each new check was confirmed to fail against a mutated page.
13. **Every page shipped a literal `%sveltekit.theme%`** (R1-13). Since the first commit, unreplaced. Gone.

## Keeping records for sale and exchange

14. **The CSV** (R1-14, part). A cell beginning `=`, `+`, `-`, `@` or a tab is written with a leading apostrophe, so a note starting "-5 °C" is text in Excel and not a formula; the lot or reference travels as a column and is shown on the plant page. The sold/traded status and a numeric price are deferred (below).
15. **Labels and sources** (R1-15). The labels page's filter reads the place and the source and matches every word ("Windowsill", "Mesa"); a filtered or searched /plants list offers "Labels for these N"; a plant's Source is a link to every plant from that source.
16. **Smaller things** (R1-16). A new place's kind is chosen, never defaulted to Room, with a sentence if it is not; a price without a source is shown; the sync page says Offline from its first frame when the browser is offline, not after the first failed run.
