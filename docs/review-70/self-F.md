# Self-review of round seventy, reviewer F: the account against the code, names and search, a first visitor

Working copy `(scratch)` at `76dcd55`; live corpus `0c2959d95b5883d2`. `/api/index` fetched once (8,947 entries). 32 species pages fetched (30 drawn at random with seed 70, plus Ixora coccinea and Ceropegia penduliflora), the front page, nine 404s and redirects, and 30 shared-name 404s. Kew's own `wcvp.zip` (4 June 2026) downloaded once and read locally. Trial fixes: `F-fix.diff` (five source files, four tests; each test fails on the base and passes with the fix; the 44 `r70*` unit files, 657 tests, pass on the base).

## Findings

**1. Ixora coccinea still shows Embothrium coccineum's range, and 70c's family rule can never fire on Kew's files. Confirmed.** Live `/api/dossier/8793122` (built 2026-10-15): native Argentina South, Chile Central, Chile South; Kew's description "shrub or tree", "temperate" (Embothrium's); the page's own Wikipedia summary says "native to Southern India, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka" two sections above "Native to: Argentina South, Chile Central, Chile South". Root cause: `inFamily` (`src/lib/dossier/authors.ts:136`) compares each row's `family`, and the bulk path (`bulk.ts:186`, `ownRows`) passes Kew's file rows, whose `family` column is the family of the name's own genus, not of the entry it is filed under. Kew's row is `103355|753843-1|Species|Invalid|Rubiaceae|…|Ixora coccinea|Comm. ex Lam.|2788267` (2788267 is Embothrium coccineum, Proteaceae). Only GBIF's copy of WCVP rewrites the family to Proteaceae, which is why the IPNI half of 70c worked (IPNI 753844-1 is right live) and the range half did not. The test `r70c-wikidata-ipni.test.ts:64` builds its "Kew file" rows from GBIF's copy (ids 2077…, status "Synonym", family "Proteaceae"), so it passes on a shape Kew's file never has. The same cause keeps Ixora off `--list-rebuild`: `legacyAccepted` and `accepted` both resolve to Embothrium, so `kewRebuildList` sees no change (the owner's open item). Fix (in the diff): `inFamily` takes a `familyOf`, and `ownRows` passes `(r) => (this.byId.get(r.acceptedId) ?? r).family`. Over Kew's whole file, nine reference names have a row filed under another family; only Ixora coccinea's backbone author reaches one, and the eight others keep their ranges with the fix. Then rebuild 8793122.

**2. Drosera montana quotes Wikipedia's "Drosera tomentosa" article. Confirmed.** Live dossier 3191221: `summary.title` "Drosera tomentosa", the text "Drosera tomentosa is a species … endemic to Brazil … Diels reduced the species to a variety of D. montana"; record "carried from build of 2026-09-29; this build: skipped", with no proof named. Round seventy (A2, R47-4) and the corpus step expected it dropped, and neither the 70b nor the 70c fill dropped it. Running `checkSummaries` today on the live dossier with the real fetcher drops it ("…is under a name that is neither this species' nor one of its older names"), so on the owner's runs it was among the 150 and 232 "not checked". Root cause: `summary-fill.ts` `checkSummaries` counts a stored summary it could not check (`c.unchecked += again.length`) and writes nothing, so an unproved summary under another title stays on the page with no record. That contradicts `/about/how` ("A summary quoted from under another title names its proof …; one whose proof could not be read … is recorded as not checked"). Fix: when a stored summary whose title is not the name stays unanswered, withhold it and record "not checked: …" (rule 3), or at least write that record. Then audit every dossier whose `summary.title` differs from its name and whose record names no proof.

**3. Older names that are another species' accepted name are shown and asserted without their author. Confirmed.** 169 pages list under "Also known as" a binomial the reference holds as another species' accepted name, with no author: Annona montana's page reads "Also known as … Annona muricata" (its record holds "Annona muricata Vell.", a homonym; Annona muricata L., the soursop, has its own page). Others: Allamanda schottii / A. cathartica, Agave deserti / A. pringlei, Adenia ballyi / A. globosa. Worse, `/species/annona-montana?was=Annona%20muricata` prints "Annona muricata is a synonym: the GBIF backbone accepts this species as Annona montana". The `?was=` guard (`+page.server.ts:159`) admits any name in the record and checks only names *shared* as older names (`ambiguousSynonym`), never accepted names. Fix (in the diff): count species of the index accepted under `was` as other holders, so the line says the name names no one species here. For "Also known as", print the record's author for such names (`+page.svelte:53` already has `d.name.synonyms` with authors).

**4. A range that disagrees with every record hides the records. Confirmed.** `+page.svelte:98`: when no record is in range, `evidence` returns "No georeferenced records inside the stated native range." before the clause that names `nOutsideRange`. Ixora coccinea (1,749 records, all outside) and Ceropegia penduliflora (49, all outside) read "Wild records: none in range" with nothing saying the records exist. The builder prints "range disagrees with records: all N outside it" (`build-dossiers.ts:1171`), but the page does not say so. Fix: in that branch, append "`N` records outside the WCVP native range not used" when `nOutsideRange > 0`, and say the range and the records disagree when every record lies outside.

**5. "Photographs not checked: iNaturalist was skipped (not asked)" on pages where iNaturalist was asked. Confirmed.** 8 of 32 sampled pages (Pseudobombax calcicola, Ceropegia gikyi, Commiphora antunesii, Massonia sempervirens, Lachenalia thomasiae, Echeveria tolucensis, Pinguicula sharpii, Ceropegia ananthagiriensis) carry the banner, while the same page's record says iNaturalist's wild and cultivated photographs answered "nothing to report" ("that build: filled 2026-09-20"). `photoSourceGaps` (`src/lib/ui/ref/photos.ts:37`) counts the `inat.taxon` row, carried as "skipped" from a rederive, as iNaturalist not asked. Separately, 25 of 32 pages say "iNaturalist taxon: not asked" although `--fill inat-ids` checked every id on 13 and 14 October. `fillInatIds` (`corpus-fills.ts:166`, `if (carried && …own…) continue;`) writes nothing for an id it checked and kept, while `/about/how` says "Every iNaturalist taxon id … is checked with iNaturalist". Fix: the banner one is in the diff (iNaturalist's answered photograph rows settle iNaturalist). The fill should write `inat.taxon` as answered, "checked <day>: active".

**6. 807 tiles show a photograph with no licence where the licence is known. Confirmed.** Every index entry whose thumbnail is an iNaturalist CC0 photograph credited "no rights reserved" has no `credit`, so the tile says "Photo: iNaturalist" (front page today: Geissorhiza aspera). `tileCreditOf` (`head.ts:158`) needs an author in `INAT_LINE`, but the credit can be said short and true as "CC0", so the about page's exception does not cover it (rule 4). Fix (in the diff): a CC0 "no rights reserved" line gives "CC0"; the index is then rebuilt.

**7. The header's group chip is unlabelled. Confirmed.** "Euphorbiaceae · Finland, Iceland +54 · cactus or succulent" heads Euphorbia peplus (petty spurge), and "· cactus or succulent" heads Pseudobombax calcicola, which Kew calls a "tree". The Cultivation section labels it as the archetype table's genus listing, but the header line (`class="vern meta"`) does not, so it reads as a fact about the plant (rule 1). Fix: "listed with cacti and succulents" or a title naming the table.

**8. `/species/echinopsis-pachanoi` redirects with no word, and search finds nothing. Confirmed.** GBIF's match sends it to Trichocereus macrogonus (through var. pachanoi); the page shows no `?was=` line, because the dossier's synonyms are the species', not the variety's, and `/api/search?q=Echinopsis%20pachanoi` answers `[]`. Fix: let `asVariety` admit a `was` the backbone resolved through a variety of this species (the redirect knows it), and say so.

**9. Sentences the code does not keep.** Confirmed unless marked.
- /about/how: "Kew's rows are read in the species' family … Kew's Ixora coccinea L. is the plant" (70c): not on Kew's files (finding 1).
- "When Wikidata does not answer, after its lag is waited out, …" (70c): only the first lagged species of a run waits (`lagSpell`, `mwWait.patient = run === 0`). Ceropegia penduliflora, built 2026-10-14T23:40 on 70c, was asked for 38 s and records "failed … 7.2 seconds lagged; the identifiers of the build of 2026-10-14 are kept". Say "after its lag is waited out, or at once within a spell of lagged species".
- "A summary quoted from under another title names its proof … recorded as not checked": not for stored summaries the fill could not check (finding 2).
- "Every iNaturalist taxon id … is checked with iNaturalist": checked, but recorded as "not asked" (finding 5).
- "A taxon GBIF or Kew holds as another species of this reference is never taken, however it was found": in `judgeTaxon`'s `lenient` path (the name search's matched term) Kew is never asked, so a Kew synonym of another species here, which GBIF does not place, is taken. Suspected; section 9 of the round half-admits it.
- The 70c clause "its epithet spelt one letter otherwise": the code (`slip`) also takes a letter moved up to three places and needs five letters; the Commons sentence says "moved", this one does not.
- Unclear: "From iNaturalist a taxon is taken only when its own name is one of the species' own names" reads against the 70b list two paragraphs on (GBIF's holding, Kew's synonymy, genus transfer, slip). Scope it to the older-name route or drop "only".

**10. The seam method drives few rules, and none of 70b's or 70c's. Confirmed.** `tests/about-pages.ts` is sound as a finder, but the two `r70p-about-*` files open 52 of about 609 sentences (290 on /how, 319 on /formats by my split). They keep 20 verbatim `toMatch` pins of sentence text and 16 regular expressions over code (`expect(raw(...)).toMatch`), the method the round says it replaced. Of the 17 sentences 70b and 70c changed, four are opened, and their new clauses are pinned verbatim, not driven. Three mutations each survive both files (51 tests pass): disabling the iNaturalist slip clause (`inat.ts:260`, `false &&`), making `inFamily` return its input, and dropping the Wikidata alias match (`wikimedia.ts:295`). Fix: one driven test per 70b/70c sentence, with the sentence's own example (Echinocereus chisosensis, Ixora coccinea on Kew-file rows, Fragaria virginiana's alias, Tricyrtis formosana var. formosana).

**11. Shared older names: same author under two holders reads as a contradiction. Confirmed, minor.** 5 of 30 sampled shared-name 404s give one author for both holders: "Vernonia praealta (L.) Michx., now Vernonia gigantea; Vernonia praealta (L.) Michx., now Vernonia noveboracensis"; likewise Chaenomeles lagenaria, Cactus kotschubeyi, Olopetalum ovatum, Amelancus canadensis. The authors are GBIF's (pro parte), but the sentence implies two names. Fix: when the authors coincide, say "GBIF files this one name under both".

**12. Search oddities a visitor meets. Confirmed, minor.** "Tecoma speciosa", an older name of Campsis radicans and held by it alone, answers Tecomanthe speciosa first, because a genus prefix makes it "a name typed as a name". /formats says that order, but N2's "an exact older name ranks first" does not hold here (1 of 36,435). "cactus" answers two Copiapoas whose headline is "Cactus". "fireweed" answers Datura metel (headline "Fireweed") before Chamaenerion angustifolium. Both follow from the headline rule.

**13. The iNaturalist slip rule ignores GBIF's word that the slipped name is another species. Suspected.** In `judgeTaxon` the slip is taken when "GBIF holds X as a species, not of this reference", that is, when GBIF exactly matches an accepted species one letter away, outside the 8,947 (rule 3). Fix: take the slip only when GBIF has no record of the name.

- **"Wikidata gives Qx as the basionym of Q278283 and Q278283"** (Tetragonia tetragonoides, Ariocarpus agavioides): `summary-proof.ts:163`. When the title's item is the species' own item (`ti.id === sp.id`), and the sitelink test failed, the basionym branch names it twice. Check `ti.id === sp.id` first and record proof `item` ("the title's Wikidata item is this species' item").
- **Rosa luciae**: no structured proof ties "Rosa lucieae" to it; `spellingKey` does not join "luciae" and "lucieae", rightly.
