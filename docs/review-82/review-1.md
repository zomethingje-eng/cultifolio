# Independent review 1 (another model, 2026-10-23, at commit 8f2b495), as the owner pasted it; condensed by the coordinator to its findings, with every figure, address and code reference kept.

Verdict: works with faults; no blocker. ~140 requests to cultifolio.com plus a data audit of 110 dossiers (2,895 photographs). Chromium only (a 489 px pane, emulated 320/375/812x375/768/1280). Code read, tests not run.

Scorecard: UX 7, appearance 7, mobile 8, data 7, code 7, performance 6, accessibility 9, honesty of claims 7.

## Major

1. **A Replace from a backup resumed by two tabs can lose photographs (rule 6, pixels only).** Traced in source, not reproduced. `promote()` sets staging-pending, wipes the live stores and copies photographs one put per transaction; every `openVault()` that sees the flag runs the same copy, with no cross-tab lock. Tab B opening mid-copy re-wipes what A copied; A finishes from its own index, clears the flag and deletes the staging DB; if B is closed first those photos are gone. Records survive. Code: `src/lib/db/vault.ts:187-195` (resume), `268-277` (flag), `291-293` (wipe), `312`, `324-327` (copy), `328-333` (clear, delete staging). Fix: a Web Lock `cultifolio-replace` around stage, promote and resume; re-check the flag inside the lock; post `replaced` as the wipe lands; an e2e test opening a second tab during a Replace with photographs.
2. **Search buries the species whose headline common name is exactly the query.** `/?q=peppermint`: 19 matches, Mentha × piperita (headline "Peppermint") 18th, after eleven Eucalyptus, an Oxalis, a Pelargonium, a Kleinia, an Agonis and an Acacia (the coordinator confirmed this live). `/?q=echeveria`: 259 species alphabetical, E. elegans (most grown, 26 photographs) outside the first 30. Code: `src/lib/core/search.ts:829-838` (`genericAlone` sets c = 3.5 for a one-word name two or more genera carry, headline included); `844` (sort ends in the alphabet). Fix: exempt the headline (`commonNames[0]`) from the demotion; for a genus word with no common-name hit, sort by photos desc before `sortKey`.
3. **Search and the index equate author-less homonyms with older names (rule 3).** `/?q=Sempervivum montanum` lists "Sempervivum tectorum · older name: Sempervivum montanum", but the dossier's synonym is S. montanum Sibth. & Sm., a different name from the page S. montanum L. The species page gets it right ("Under another author… not a species of the reference"); the index strips authors. Index-wide: 1,691 of 48,361 pages carry in `syn[]` a bare binomial that is another page's accepted name (1,802 pairs); 1,413 in `older[]`. 6 of 6 inspected are homonyms. Code: `src/lib/dossier/index-entry.ts` (syn/older written author-less). Fix: filter `syn[]`/`older[]` against the set of accepted names at index build; keep those with a differing author under a marked key the search can word.
4. **Echeveria peacockii is wired to Dudleya pulverulenta's Kew entry, and one Kew taxon has two pages.** `/api/dossier/g7530398` has `name.kew.entry` 2778991, the entry of page k2778991 Dudleya pulverulenta; `kew.synonyms` list "Dudleya pulverulenta"; the POWO link goes there. The page's range is Kew's Echeveria desmetiana "reached by older names", while k2781125 Echeveria desmetiana is that reference page: one Kew entry, two pages. GBIF lumps them through the later homonym Cotyledon pulverulenta Baker. `scripts/merged-pages.txt` records "Echeveria peacockii into Dudleya pulverulenta". Should: E. peacockii merges into E. desmetiana (POWO's synonymy); a page never carries another page's Kew entry. Code: `src/lib/dossier/bulk.ts:439-461` (`viaOlderNames` reads heterotypic synonyms, "Same-type names are not required"); `scripts/merged-pages.txt`; compare `src/lib/dossier/extra-keys.ts:9-25` and `sources/gbif.ts:110` (`sameTypeNames`). Fix: restrict or guard `viaOlderNames`; refuse a Kew entry another page holds; correct the merge line; a predeploy check that no two pages share `name.kew.entry`.
5. **iNaturalist taxon accepted on a GBIF FUZZY match.** `src/lib/dossier/sources/inat.ts:338-344`. **Fixed already (r82base).**
6. **A Kew-id page was matched on Wikidata by its label although /about/how says it never is (rule 5).** `/api/dossier/k2781134` (built 2026-10-22): `upstream.wikidata.detail` "found by its label; its IPNI identifier 86934-2 is Kew's…". /about/how: a page with a Kew id "is matched by those identifiers and never by its label". Wikidata's P846 on that item is 7334640; the dossier's GBIF id is 8315197. Code: `src/lib/dossier/build.ts:315, 470`; `scripts/build-dossiers.ts:2170-2172`; `sources/wikimedia.ts:317-416` (340 writes the words). Fix: load species-list.txt for `entryOf` in `--keys` builds; re-prove k-pages whose record says "found by its label"; or amend the sentence.
7. **267 of 565 non-ok upstream rows in the 110 sampled dossiers carry no reason.** inat.photos.cultivated skipped 109/109 without detail, inat.photos.wild skipped 36/36, inat.taxon skipped 21/21 (these await the fill), wikipedia none 36/40, commons none 32/32, gbif.media none 29/29, wikidata none 2/9, wcvp.distribution none 1/2 (g7281739 Bisnaga hamatacantha: `distribution.source` "not available", 461 open records, no range and no reason). `src/lib/dossier/build.ts:647-649` writes INAT_LEFT_TO_FILL at HEAD. Coordinator's reading: most are iNaturalist rows awaiting the fill, and "none" is an answer; the remainder (Bisnaga's range row and the like) is the real fault.
8. **Species pages are heavy and the service worker's caches grow without bound.** `/species/sempervivum-tectorum` 238,280 B HTML (47 kB gzipped): 84.5 kB inline SVG (8 charts, two maps, the world coastline inlined per page), 65.4 kB hydration data, 25.6 kB inline CSS, 45 script chunks; TTFB 0.36-1.0 s uncached. Three Cache API caches after a session: `cultifolio-corpus`, the current build, one from 2026-10-04 never pruned. Code: `src/lib/map/still.ts:29-31, 88-90`; `src/service-worker.ts:90-93` (keeps this build and the previous), `226-252` (no eviction). (Round 82: the cache half only.)
9. **`/api/index` (37.4 MB) 6 times per 10 min per address; a /24 of IPv4 addresses has no network window.** `limited()` adds a /48 window for IPv6 only. Code: `src/lib/server/sync.ts:1767-1777, 1672-1676`; `src/routes/api/index/+server.ts:22`. Fix: `upstreamNetwork(ip)` with `NET_FACTOR` in `limited()`.
