# Review round thirty-five: two reviews of the deployed `d506c4f`, triaged

The second reviewer's round twenty-three (seven findings) and the first reviewer's round thirty-one (thirteen, in depth, with the live reference walked through a fetch tool). Both reviewed what rounds thirty-two and thirty-three deployed. Every finding was checked in the source or reproduced. "R1-n" is the first reviewer's; "R2-n" the second's. Both put the same thing first, and it is mine: round thirty-three's required set.

## The fold rule, finished

1. **The required set was what the pages threw on, not what the app assumes** (R2-1; R1 verified the rule and found its readers). I had narrowed it to make round thirty-three's fixtures pass, so a batch without its count folded live, and `10 > undefined` is false: the pot guard let any number through. The set is the types' own non-optional fields now (a plant's name and status; a batch's name, method, date, count and status; a photograph's day, size and bytes; the rest as before), and a table-driven test damages each required field of each kind, proves the record waits, and proves a later readable value makes it whole.
2. **Two readers still took "not removed" as live** (R1-2, reproduced). `rawParent` and `nearestLive` in the store, and `locPath` in the backup writer, so an incomplete place hid the complete places and plants under it from the tree, the counts and the CSV ("House › undefined"). All three go through `isComplete`; an incomplete place is walked past like a removed one.
3. **`potUp` made a false claim of wild-collected seed** (R1-1; the reviewer's one thing to fix). A method this build does not know was treated as seed, so an offset batch with wild provenance potted up to plants "raised from wild-collected seed" (`f1`, `sourceForm: seedling`), written to the log and synced. An unknown method now gives its plants an unknown provenance and no source form. And `OLD_METHODS` is made by the importer's own rule over the labels (lower-cased, one trailing "s" cut), so "offsets / pup", the spelling it wrote, is on the list; the test checks that spelling. Unit tests for both.
4. **A restore deleted the pixels of a waiting photograph** (R1-3). The post-merge cleanup removed pixels of any photograph `photo()` did not return, which since round thirty-three includes a waiting one. Only a removed photograph's pixels go; a waiting one keeps them for the day a batch completes it.
5. **Counts that included records the pages do not show** (R1-4). The restore preview said "7 plants" while 6 appeared; it counts whole records now and names the waiting ones. The waiting line on the sync page shows whenever the count is above zero, not only beside a set-aside batch.
6. **Edit forms wrote claims the grower never made** (R1-5, both reproduced). The place form carries an unknown kind as an extra option, as the plant and batch forms did since round thirty-three. A price edit on a plant that had no `nameKind` or `provenance` wrote `species` and `unknown`; a field the record never had, left on the form's default, stays unset, on the plant and the batch forms.
7. **The backup reader could be walked around by entry name** (R1-6, R2-4; both reproduced: eight deflated entries alternating the two sheet names at 250 MB inflated two gigabytes). The sheets are never inflated (nothing reads them back), the manifest and device settings have a 4 MB cap, the log keeps its 192 MB cap, the photographs are bounded by the file's size as before, and a name seen twice is refused. Test: the sheet bomb is not touched; a 5 MB manifest is not ours.

## The reference

8. **A written trailing zero cannot survive a JSON number** (R2-2). Round thirty-three counted decimals on the raw value, but the raw value is a number by then, and "-33.120" is two decimals as a number. The download's parser counts the decimals from the text and carries the count, and a publisher's `coordinatePrecision` of 0.001° or finer counts as three; the count from a number stays as a floor on the API path (it undercounts, never overstates). Tests through the parser, the index and a build.
9. **Rank markers in the query** (R2-3). "haworthia pumila var margaritifera", the label as written, found nothing while "haworthia pumila margaritifera" did. The query's markers go the way the index's did. Tests with var., subsp., ssp. and f.
10. **Duplicate photographs** (R1-9; Gymnocalycium mihanovichii showed three iNaturalist pictures twice, from iNaturalist and via GBIF). One copy by iNaturalist photograph id, the iNaturalist record kept for its cultivated flag, at the build and as a dossier is read, so the live pages are right on deploy. Test.
11. **The hemisphere notice** (R2-5, R1-8). Round thirty-four reworded it; the figure was still the poleward count. The split records the equatorial cells, and the notice prints the cells the envelope reads and which were left out.
12. **The evidence sentence** (R1-7). "The climate envelope on the 533 of them placed to within 10 km" counted the records in the land cells as if they were all the well-placed ones; it says "the 533 of them in the land cells it reads, of 581 placed to within 10 km".
13. **A sea cell's night was still the headline** (R1-11; Melocactus intortus, 23.7 °C at a cell 12% land). A figure the page itself says is the sea's is nearer a refusal than a measurement. As a dossier is read, extremes from a POWER cell under half land are set aside (`extremesSea`, status `sea`); the cold floor is the range's coldest mean night, the card says why, and the notice beneath says what the sea cell gave. No rebuild needed. Test.
14. **A variety's old name arrived silently** (R1-12). `trichocereus-pachanoi` reached Trichocereus macrogonus with no line, because the backbone files the name under a variety and the guard wanted the species' own list. The binomial of a listed variety counts, and the line says "an older name of a variety of this species".
15. **The carried-row chain** (R1-10). Each rederive wrapped the previous row's "carried from build of…", nine deep on Albuca yerburyi. A carried row keeps the build that asked.
16. **Image sizes** (R2-7, R1-13). The strip's `srcset` let a phone's density promote every 150 px tile to the 500 px file, and one featured tile was a 3.96 MB animated GIF: the phone home page weighed 7.2 MB against the desktop's 0.8. One size by surface now: the strip at `small` (240 px), the group rows at `square` (75 px), the hero keeps its `srcset`; a still image is preferred over a GIF for the hero and the tile wherever one exists; a `srcset` declares the host's true widths (GBIF's 160 and 400, not iNaturalist's) and is omitted for a host with no sizes. The live check fetches every photograph the home page names and fails on one over 300 kB or 1.5 MB together, so the daily rotation cannot do this again unseen.
17. **Smaller** (R1-13). A species 404 is cached five minutes (it had no cache header). The raw synonym string ("Aloe radula KerGawl.") was not reproduced: the page cuts every synonym to its binomial, and the fetch tool the reviewer used paraphrases; if it shows again, the page it was on is what I need.
