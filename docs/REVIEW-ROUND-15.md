# Review round fifteen: two reviews of `5033dd9`, triaged

The first reviewer went wide again on the round-fourteen build: fifteen findings and two design notes, with the live site checked for the figures (every cold-floor figure agreed across the glance, the cards, the note and the label wherever the species has an extremes series) and round fourteen's seven fixes verified. The second reviewer found one thing, on the labels page's wording. Every finding was checked in the source; all sixteen are real and done here, and both design notes are answered, one by a fix and one by a decision. "n" is the first review's numbering; the second review's one finding is item 17. Three of the sixteen are faults in earlier rounds' fixes (1, 3 and 6), and are said to be.

## Data lost or wrong

1. **Importing a v2 file brought every removed plant back, nameless** (1; the reviewer's first impression for anyone with a v2 collection). `ingest` stamped `importedOn` on every imported plant without one, tombstones included, and an edit after a removal undoes the removal, which is the rule round thirteen's tombstone stamping relies on. One line: only a record that is not removed gets the stamp. Store test: a file with one live plant and one tombstone gives one plant after the import and after a reload; fails against the old line.
2. **"Stop syncing" and "Replace from backup" did not reach a run in flight or another tab** (2). A running push or pull held its own meta and wrote it back, key included, after `forget()` had cleared it, so the device was syncing again after a reload with an outbox `forget()` had emptied; another tab's engine stayed configured; and `promote()` never announced, so other tabs folded the old log and a note saved there wrote a record with `notes` and nothing else. Fixed in `src/lib/sync/engine.svelte.ts` and `src/lib/db/vault.ts`: every meta write goes through one `save(m)` that refuses when the engine's meta is no longer the one the run holds, so a run interrupted by "Stop syncing" cannot write the old key back; `forget()` announces `sync-forgotten` and every other tab drops its configuration; `promote()` announces `replaced` and every other tab reloads onto the new log. Engine test: forget during a run, and the stored meta stays null.
3. **Two devices importing one v2 file could put different changes under one stamp, and a pull replaced one with the other on disk** (3; a fault in round twelve's importer). All imports were stamped as one writer `v2imp` from one shared counter, so a device that already had a record gave the skipped record's stamps to the next one, and the vault stored a pulled change by stamp alone, replacing a local change with different content. Two fixes: each imported record is stamped under its own writer (`v2imp` plus a hash of its kind and id, within the sixteen characters the stamp allows), with its own counter, so no two records can share a stamp whatever either device already holds; and a change arriving from the server whose stamp is already stored with other content is left alone, with a warning in the console, so a stored change is never silently replaced. Import test: two records' stamps differ by writer even at one millisecond; the tombstone's number is stamped under the record's own writer.
4. **The duplicate-number repair numbered a plant into its acquisition year** (14). A plant minted 2026-0001 offline with `acquired: 2019-05-01` was repaired to 2019-0001. The year is now read from the number being replaced. Store test.

## What a page said

5. **The plant page shifted the growing months by the wrong hemisphere when no site was set** (4). The species page, compare and labels fall back to the first bench with coordinates; the plant page skipped that step. Same fallback now.
6. **A label could not tell "climate not checked" from "no climate"** (5). The care line never saw the climate status, so a species whose climate source refused printed the same blank as one with no climate, and the page did not count it. `careLine` takes the status and returns "climate not checked" for a refusal; the labels page passes it and says how many labels print it and why, which is not a statement that the species has no climate.
7. **A bench with a sub-zero floor said "frost: clear" over a frost night** (11). With a floor set, any forecast that did not reach it became level `none`, so a -5 °C floor over a -2 °C night showed "frost: clear" and "All clear." where the frost page, from the same forecast, said "Frost forecast". A floor the forecast does not reach no longer clears it: a frost or cold night keeps its own level and the floor sentence is added to it. The pill says "reaches the floor", as the sentence has since round thirteen. e2e: a -5 °C floor over a -2 °C night says "Frost forecast." and "above the -5.0 °C floor", and no pill says clear.
8. **One temperature printed at two precisions on one page** (12). The bench floor pill and card, the plant page's "bottom out at", the batch page's bottom heat and the at-a-glance cold figure for a species without an extremes series printed no decimals where the sentence beside them printed one; a 2.5 °C floor was "3 °C" in three places. One decimal at every one of those sites; the e2e checks the floor pill.
9. **Frost times and forecast days could be two or three hours off, under a comment claiming an hour** (13). The offset is the site's longitude in whole hours, which is mean solar time to the hour and not the clock: Mendoza (UTC-3, 68.8° W) rounds to -5, western China and Spain in summer are similar. MET's compact answer carries no zone, and a zone table is more than a frost line needs, so the times are now labelled for what they are: "around 05:00 Thursday solar time", "between 21:00 Wednesday and 03:00 Thursday solar time". The comment says the same. Unit tests on both wordings.
10. **The label dropped "not shifted"** (15). When the habitat's year cannot be shifted (a flat curve, or within 10° of the equator) the label printed the habitat's months with nothing to say so, and a reader on the other side of the equator would take them as their own. The months carry "(hab.)" in that case, only when the reader is on the other side.

## Sync that stopped

11. **After any deploy, a photograph that was ever set aside stopped sync on that device for good** (6; the reviewer's one thing to fix, and a fault in round fourteen's retry). Photographs and batches share the quarantine, and round fourteen's retry handed every other-build entry to the batch fetch, which failed on a photo id and threw, before the number repair and the photo pull, on every run from the next deploy on. Each quarantine entry now says what it is; the batch retry takes only batches, and a photograph set aside by an older build is tried again in the photo pull. Engine test: a photo entry from an older build, the run completes, the sync time moves, and `/api/sync/log/<photo id>` is never asked for.
12. **A retry that failed ended the run** (the reviewer's second design note). One bad key should not stop the number repair or the photo pull. A retry that throws is now noted in the console and the run goes on; only the server's "wait" is rethrown. Engine test: a 503 on the retry, the entry stays, the run finishes.
13. **A 429 on push stopped the pull, so a device with anything pending received nothing until the quota reset** (7). After the address's day allowance was spent, every run sent one POST, got 429 and stopped; other devices' changes waited for UTC midnight. The push's "wait" is held, the pull runs, the sync time moves, and the wait is reported afterwards with "received" in front, so the status says both what arrived and what is waiting. Engine test: push limited, the other device's record arrives, the error carries the wait.
14. **"Vault full" caused by a photograph never cleared while the outbox was empty** (8). The flag was cleared only after a batch push succeeded, and with nothing to push none was attempted. When the flag is set the photo push tries once, and a success clears it.

## Forms that went quiet

15. **The add, new-batch and pot-up forms went silent when a write was refused** (9). `busy` was never reset when the write threw, and only the plants pages showed the write error. `try/finally` on all three, and the same write-error notice.
16. **The pot-up and new-batch number fields took blanks and fractions** (10). A cleared pot-up box exited before any message; 2.5 was saved as a batch's seed count and a later count of 3 refused as more than went in. Both go through `numberOrNull` and are floored, as the add form has since round thirteen; a figure that was changed is said.
17. **The labels notice said the labels say "not checked" when the paper is blank** (the second review's one finding). Round fourteen took the line off the paper and left the sentence saying it was on it. The notice now says the preview marks those care lines and the printed labels leave them blank.
