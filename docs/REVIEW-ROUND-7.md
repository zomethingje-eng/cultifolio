# Review round seven: two outside reviews of `5ab87b7`, triaged

Two models reviewed the deployed build against the live site and the source, with the same brief (code, user experience, health and look; findings only with a reproduction; nothing from rounds six or the agent walk re-reported). The first returned 18 findings, the second 24. Every one was checked against the source before it counted. This file lists them once, merged where they overlap, in the order they were fixed: data integrity first, then flows that block or silently do the wrong thing, then confusion, then presentation. "R1-n" and "R2-n" name the finding in each review. Everything marked fixed is in this commit with a test where the finding was a behaviour.

## Data integrity and security

1. **Two tabs of one browser gave two plants the same accession number** (R1-1; R2-2 is the same allocator seen through a replace-from-backup). The number was chosen from the tab's own picture of the collection and written with an ordinary append; nothing crossed tabs, and a replace from an older backup forgot every number issued since. Fixed in `src/lib/db/vault.ts` and `src/lib/db/collection.svelte.ts`: the vault keeps a ledger of every number ever written on the device in `meta` (`issued:accession`, `issued:sowing`), every append adds the numbers its changes carry, and a write that mints numbers (`appendChangesClaiming`) reads the ledger and chooses inside the same read-write transaction that stores the change, which IndexedDB serialises across tabs. The ledger survives a replace (only `changes`, `photos` and `outbox` are cleared), so a number given after the backup's date stays given. A `BroadcastChannel` tells the other tabs, which fold in what they have not seen. e2e: "two tabs adding at once get two numbers".
2. **An interrupted replace could merge the old vault under the restored one** (R1-2). `promote()` set the flag, cleared, copied; a stop between the flag and the clear meant the recovery copied on top of the untouched old log. Fixed: the flag means "staging is the collection now", and every run of the recovery clears before it copies.
3. **The clock refused its own future stamps after a reload** (R1-3). `observe()` rejected any stamp more than five minutes ahead, including one this device made while its clock was fast; after a correction and reload, a new edit lost to the old value. Fixed in `src/lib/core/hlc.ts`: the ahead limit applies to other devices only. Unit test added.
4. **A lost photo-upload reply became a permanent refusal** (R1-7). The retry re-sealed with a fresh nonce, the server answered 409 for different bytes under a held id, and the client marked the photo refused though the server held it. Fixed in `src/lib/sync/engine.svelte.ts`: on 409 the client fetches what the server holds, decrypts it and compares the pixels; equal means pushed. Unit test added.
5. **The removal of a germination count could leave "0 up, 2 potted"** (R1-5). Fixed on the batch page: a count is removable only if the counts that remain still cover what was potted and lost; otherwise the row says "kept: the potted plants rest on it". e2e added.
6. **"Cold floor" meant two figures on one page, and the label printed the archetype minimum as "hab. night"** (R1-6, R2-1; the reviewers' one-thing-to-fix). `coldFloor()` returned one number with a boolean; the glance card showed the habitat night under the same label the sheet used for the raised floor; `careLine()` trusted the boolean. Fixed in `src/lib/core/sheet.ts` (`ColdFloor` carries `habitat`, `floor`, `raised`, `group`; `cultivationSheet` returns the resolved floor), `src/lib/core/note.ts` (the label prints `hab. night` for the habitat's own figure and adds `group min` when the table raised the floor), the species glance card, the compare row and the share card, which all show the sheet's floor and say when the table raised it. Unit tests updated; e2e checks the card and the sheet agree.
7. **JSON-LD was injected without escaping `<`** (R2-8). A vernacular name is user-contributed upstream. Fixed: `<` becomes `<`. And a Content Security Policy is now sent (`svelte.config.js`): scripts from this origin only (Kit's inline script and Svelte's `this.__e=event` replay handler by hash, plus app.html's theme line), styles from self with inline attributes, images over https, connections to self, no framing. "No third-party scripts" is now a header, not a sentence.
8. **The server held an unkeyed SHA-256 of every batch's plaintext and the millisecond of every last edit** (R2-15). Batches are now named by the hour of their last change, a fixed counter, the device, and twelve digits of an HMAC-SHA-256 fingerprint under a key derived from the vault key (`info: name`); the full fingerprint replaces the plain hash in `X-Batch-Plain`, so the re-send check still works and the server holds nothing a guess can be tested against. Old names stay valid. `/about/formats` says what remains visible (sizes, arrival times, the hour and device of each batch) and no longer implies zero knowledge; the README says "end-to-end encrypted" instead.
9. **A syntactically valid but empty MET reply became "No forecast available"** (R1-10). Fixed in `/api/forecast`: no days, or no finite minimum, is the source not answering (502, no-store). **The route had no rate limit and turned a bad `alt` into "source did not answer"** (R2-10). Fixed: a `forecast` bucket (60 per ten minutes per address), `alt` a number in −500 to 9000 rounded to 10 m in the cache key, anything else a 400.
10. **Day counters parsed a local date as UTC midnight** (R1-12, R2-3). Fixed: `daysBetween()` in `src/lib/core/dates.ts` compares calendar dates; the plant page, plants list and bench page use it. Unit test added.
11. **Editing a plant's species left the old species' climate on screen** (R1-4). The dossier effect kept the first dossier it had. Fixed: the effect keys on the name and key, resets, and drops a late answer for a name no longer on the record. e2e added. While there: **the plant page pulled the 3 MB index to find one dossier** (R2-11); it now asks `/api/dossier/<key>` by the record's own key (the file the offline worker caches) and falls back to the index only when the key leads nowhere.
12. **Event forms announced success before the write** (R1-9). Fixed: the submit awaits the write and closes only when it resolves; a refused write keeps the form open under the page's write-error notice.
13. **The evidence line said the climate rested on every in-range record; the climate section said ten** (R2-4). Fixed: the sentence gives both counts when they differ (the marker on all records, the envelope on those placed within 10 km), and a climate read from under a dozen records carries the "indicative" warning.
14. **Three quarters of species listed a GBIF occurrence download as a paper** (R2-5). Fixed at source (`type:!dataset` in the OpenAlex filter and a drop of DOIs under `10.15468/`) and at render, so the corpus on disk is clean until the next `--fill`. Unit test.
15. **Negative zero printed as "−0 °C"** (R2-6). Fixed: `fixed()` in `src/lib/core/units.ts`, used by `temp`, `tempN` and the monthly table. Unit test.
16. **Genera beginning with X lost their first letter** (R1-11). `genusOf` stripped a leading "x" as a hybrid sign. Fixed: only a standalone "x " or "×" is a sign. Unit test.

## Flows

17. **Returning visitors stayed on an old build for days** (R2-7). No `skipWaiting`, and an installed app never closes its last tab. Fixed: when the version poll sees a deploy the layout tells the waiting worker to take over and reloads under it; the worker keeps the previous build's cache for one generation so a tab still on the old build finds its chunks. **Settings (and the other units-varying pages) were blank offline after a units switch** (R1-8): the cache honoured `Vary: cookie`. Fixed: offline fallbacks ignore Vary; the page re-reads its units on hydration. **Plant pages opened from a label waited on the network with no timeout** (R2-13). Fixed: a detail page under `/plants`, `/benches` or `/sowings` is served its section's shell from the cache without asking the network. **The worker script listed 19,474 files** (R2-24): the corpus is filtered out at build (`kit.serviceWorker.files`) and lookups use Sets.
18. **The picker offered infraspecific names without their rank, some twice, and filed a plant with no species page while its button linked to a 404** (R2-9). Fixed: suggestions show "subspecies", "variety", "form"; duplicates by name and rank collapse to one; a plant named below species reads its species' page (`speciesOf()`); the "Species page" button renders only when the reference answered. The plant's Edit form now uses the picker too (agent walk, B7).
19. **The plants list scanned every record once per plant, several times per render** (R2-12). Fixed: events and photographs are grouped by plant once per change (`$derived` maps in the store).
20. **Bottom heat accepted 77 °C** (R2-14). Fixed: outside 5–45 °C is refused with a sentence, and a figure that reads as Fahrenheit says so.
21. **Every plant entered from an existing collection was overdue on day one** (R2-17). The acquisition date stood in for the last watering and the last sighting. Fixed: a never-watered plant counts from the day its record was made (read from the record id's timestamp), the row says "no watering recorded", and `lastSeen` uses the same day rather than the acquisition date.
22. **The home search could not find a plant** (R2-18). Fixed: a query that matches a number, field number, name-as-received or cultivar lists the grower's own plants above the catalogue, and Enter opens the first.
23. **A new place silently went inside the place currently picked** (R1-13). Fixed: "New…" proposes the picked place's parent (another bench in the same greenhouse), and the form shows the resulting path.

## Confusing

24. **"7 wet months" rested on an unstated rule** (R1-14, R2-19). Fixed: "7 months over 25 mm (1 in)" on the glance card, the compare row and the share card.
25. **One thing had three names** (R2-20). Fixed: "place" everywhere the interface names it (the tab stays "Benches").
26. **The authorship promise was broader than the page** (R1-15). Fixed: "except the quoted passages, which are marked and credited: Wikipedia, and where a register such as Kew's gives one, its own description".

## Health and look

27. **Prose links told apart by hue alone; chip counts at 3:1** (R1-16, R2-21). Fixed: links inside running text carry a light underline (full on hover); the counts drop their opacity.
28. **The species hero fetched the original (1 MB) and genus rows fetched 500-pixel thumbnails for 48-pixel boxes** (R1-17). Fixed: `photoAt()` in `src/lib/dossier/photo-size.ts` asks iNaturalist and the GBIF cache for the size the place needs (hero: large; rows: small); other hosts are left alone. Unit test.
29. **The plant page shifted by 0.66** (R1-18). Fixed: the page keeps its shape before the vault opens (a skeleton with the hero's and card's heights), the hero has a fixed height whether it holds the species' photograph, a stand-in or nothing, and the species image is drawn into that box.
30. **At 200% zoom a third of the screen was chrome** (R2-22). Fixed: under 480 px tall the section row and tool row stop sticking and the bottom bar hides.

## After the fixes

`npx svelte-check --threshold warning` clean; `npx vitest run` 31 files, 275 tests; `npx playwright test tests/e2e/smoke.spec.ts --workers=1` 54 tests. The two changes a deployer should know about: the CSP header (anything that later adds a third-party script or an inline `<script>` in a component must be added to `svelte.config.js` by hash), and the service worker's take-over on deploy (a returning tab will reload itself once, within five minutes of a deploy).
