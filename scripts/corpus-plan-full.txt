# The build. From files: the occurrences and GBIF's photographs (bulk\occurrence.zip, and media.zip beside an SQL
# download), Kew's range and names (bulk\wcvp_*.csv), the climate (climate\), the institutions (scripts/institutions.txt,
# agent Q's rule). Asked a species at a time: GBIF's species record, synonyms and common names (0.25 s each); Commons for
# a page with fewer than twelve photographs; NASA POWER for a new climate cell (cached for ever). In batches: Wikidata and
# Wikipedia. Nothing of OpenAlex, nothing of iNaturalist (--skip inat: the two fills below). About 1.4 s a species (agent
# B's measure, round seventy-eight): about 19 hours; plus reading the download first (the DWCA about an hour, at round
# seventy-two's 12.7 GB in 11 minutes; the SQL download about ten minutes); plus up to about 12 hours of POWER's new
# cells (at most 0.47 a species at 2.1 s a cell, the scaling study's bound). Keep the PC awake; if it stops, the same
# command goes on, and every species built is kept.
# --renew (round eighty, X): every page built before the day GBIF made the download is built again, round seventy-eight's
# 8,945 among them (without it they were kept as they were, with round seventy-two's records and none of this round's
# rules); a page built since is kept, so after a stop the same command goes on.
dossier scripts/species-list.txt --grid climate --bulk bulk --skip inat --renew
review corpus-step.log, the build's lines: "GBIF occurrences from bulk/occurrence.zip …" naming the download (an SQL download's <key>.csv, or occurrence.txt) and "… rows → … taxa"; the photographs' line ("GBIF photographs from multimedia.txt", or the images' download media.zip beside an SQL download; if it says GBIF's photographs are not asked, run npm run dossier -- --fill gbif --bulk bulk before going on); "a species list of 48357 species: 46022 built by their GBIF key, 2335 with no GBIF key in the list … built from Kew's file" and "2335 species without a GBIF key read from Kew's file", then "1415 of them read GBIF's records under 1956 keys Kew's synonymy gives them (scripts/extra-keys-kew.txt); the other 920 say GBIF's records not checked"; "Kew's names: … answered from Kew's file"; "<n> built, … kept", n about 48,357; the paragraph "Institution rule (Wikidata's 6656 botanic gardens, herbaria and zoos, 1 km): …", records left out on about a third of the species with records (2,651 of 7,776 on round seventy-seven's corpus), its list of species that lost their habitat climate read whole if it names more than a few dozen; drops.txt, if there, lists what pages built before lost.
