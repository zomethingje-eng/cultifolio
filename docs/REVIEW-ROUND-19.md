# Round nineteen: improvements before the post, from two reviews and one list of my own

Not a defect round. Two reviewers were asked what they would improve in the app as it stands at `613560b`, and their two answers were read against each other and against a list of my own; what all three agreed on, and what I agreed with in either, was ordered with the launch in mind and done here. What was refused is at the end, with why. The second half of the agreed list (designed placeholders, Frost folded into Places, contextual setup, "Protect this collection", the `Run` refactor of the sync engine, build-time homonyms, a Commons fill for the photo-less species, the wrangler update) waits until after the post. In the order they were done.

## Protecting launch day

1. **Three ceilings on new vaults.** There was one: twenty new vaults per address per day. One address at twenty is not what a front-page day looks like; a thousand addresses at one each is, and nothing bounded that. Now: five per address per day (a 429, "too many new vaults from this address today"); two hundred a day for everyone and two thousand in all, both raisable without a deploy through `SYNC_VAULTS_PER_DAY` and `SYNC_VAULTS_MAX` on the Worker; a shared ceiling answers 503 with a plain sentence, "Sync is full for now. Your collection stays on this device; try again another day.", which the sync page now shows as it is (it used to show "The server said 503"). Joining an existing vault is never refused by a ceiling. The counters are KV reads and writes, not atomic, so a burst can pass a ceiling by a few; that is bounding, not accounting, and the comment says so. Fails closed as before: no KV, or a KV that cannot be read or written, refuses every creation. Server tests: the address cap and the two shared ceilings, each from the Worker variables, the day counter fresh the next day, the total not; engine test: the 503 with `no-store`, the sentence on the client, the address sentence, a join under a met ceiling. `docs/DEPLOY.md` has the three figures and the KV keys that hold the running totals.
2. **The live check after every deploy.** `scripts/live-check.mjs` runs at the end of `npm run deploy`, against `https://cultifolio.com`: the name service three times (200 each, and whether the edge answered, which is the request that was a 500 live in round seventeen while both suites were green); the two headers on a prerendered page, a species page, the offline page and an API route; the front page with an HTML Accept, no analytics beacon and the referrer meta present; the reference under a stale corpus id answered `no-store`; a forecast; and the vault route's 400 on a JSON null. Plain Node, no dependencies, exits 1 on the first failure with the request and what came back; `npm run live-check` runs it alone, and `node scripts/live-check.mjs https://<worker>.workers.dev` runs it against a preview. `LIVE_CHECK_SKIP=names,forecast` is for a local `wrangler dev` with no upstream, never for the site. Run here against `wrangler dev` with the fixture corpus: the seven checks that need no upstream pass, and the name check fails loudly when GBIF is not reachable, as it should.

## Names

3. **Benches → Places, Sowings → Propagation.** Both reviewers, independently: a grower with a windowsill does not have a bench, and "sowings" does not cover cuttings and offsets, which the page has always tracked. The tab, the menu, the headings, the titles, the crumbs, the setup line on a plant ("the greenhouse, bench, shelf or windowsill it lives on"), the sub-heads ("a greenhouse, a bench, a shelf or a windowsill"), the offline page, the backup counts and the front page's Today line ("2 propagation batches in the tray") say the new names; "batch" is the noun inside Propagation ("New batch", "No batch with this number"); "sow", "sown" and "Sow seed" stay, being the verb. The paths moved too, `/places` and `/propagation`, because a tab called Places at `/benches` is the kind of seam a reviewer finds first; a bookmark or an installed app's cached shell at the old path gets a 301 with its query kept (`/sowings/new?loc=k1` → `/propagation/new?loc=k1`), from the hook, and a path that merely begins with the old word is not touched. The record kinds (`sowing`, `location`), the `bench` location type and every field name are unchanged, so no backup, vault or the formats page changes; the formats page says in one clause that a `sowing` is what the app calls a propagation batch. Hook unit test and an e2e for the redirects; the README and the launch draft use the new words.

## The first screen

4. **A plant without a photograph.** The page opened on a 260-pixel empty hero holding an add-photo row and a paragraph about the reference's photograph, with the plant's name below the fold on a phone. Now there is no hero unless there is a picture: the card comes first, a 96-pixel dashed tile where the photograph would go (the camera on a phone, the file chooser elsewhere; the full control is still on the photographs section), the name beside it, and the reference's photograph offered as one line, "Show the reference's photograph of this species", whose disclosure (which host sees what) and the switch open on tap. On a phone the tile is 80 pixels, the number takes its own line above the name, and the actions row drops under both. The skeleton before the vault opens has the same shape, so a plant without a photograph, which is most of them, does not jump; one with a photograph grows a hero above the card when the record arrives.
5. **One mark for "not checked".** The species head, the plant page's rain-season card, the labels notices, compare and the frost page each said "not checked" in their own way and most followed it with "which is not a statement that…" in full. `NotChecked.svelte` is the one mark: a dashed pill reading "Climate not checked" (or Photographs, Alerts, or bare), the reason on tap ("Occurrence source did not answer.", "The species reference could not be reached from here.") followed once by the rule, "Not asked, or not answered, is not 'none': nothing here claims there is none", and a link to the refusal section of `/about/how`, which now describes the mark. The section bodies keep their sentences, because there the reason is the content; the mark is for the places with no room for a sentence. The species head's "Climate known" pill is gone with it: what is known is shown, not announced.
6. **The frost line in clock time.** "Around 05:00 Thursday solar time" was true and unhelpful to a grower standing at the site. The server still says solar time by longitude, because MET's answer carries no zone. On the device, when the browser's zone is within two hours of the site's solar offset, the reader is at the site or in its zone, and the line is re-said in their clock ("around 05:00 Thursday", weekday from the same zone) before it is shown; a browser five zones away keeps the solar wording, which is at least true. The rewrite is on the way out of `getForecast`, so the cached answer is never changed and a wrong guess costs one line. Unit test at Mendoza (solar -5, clock -3): the clock line, the London browser left with solar time, a six-hour span across midnight naming both days, a warning line passed through untouched.
7. **Fewer pills, one primary action, the pledge as one line.** On the species head, pills now say only a state that is not the usual one (climate pending, not checked, none); the photograph count, the archetype group and "you grow N" are plain text, the last being what the "Yours:" line already said. On the plant card: the status pill only when the plant is not growing, the place as text in the line under the name ("at Cold frame", a link), hybrid or cultivar as a small mark in the same line; Edit, Label and Propagate behind a "···" menu (Escape, a tap outside, and the choice all close it, focus returns to the button) beside the one link, Species page; the verb bar beneath is unchanged. The pledge under the species name is one line: "Every figure here is derived from public data by a stated rule and names its source; nothing is written by a person or a model except the marked, credited quotations. How →".

## Claims

8. **What the site says about itself.** `/about/formats` said the corpus "will be published as a dataset", which is a promise; it now says what is true, that `npm run export` in the repository packages a built corpus as a dataset bundle with the licence terms per source. The README's line about "two full adversarial reviews" now points at all fifteen rounds, says they were written by language models given the repository, the live site and a written brief, that every finding was verified in the source before anything changed, and that the ones that were wrong are marked as such. The launch draft says the same in one sentence and puts the first load up front: on this build, `scripts/dev/first-load.mjs` (new; run against `wrangler dev`) reads the front page at about 98 kB of script and style compressed plus 160 kB of fonts, and a species page at 123 kB plus the same fonts, served rendered; the draft's figures are in brackets and the pre-post list says to read them again from the build that is posted.

## Weight

9. **The service worker installs less.** Every font subset (nine files, 248 kB) was cached at install. Now the Latin subsets of the three fonts are (four files, 164 kB, every glyph the pages set in them), and the Latin-extended and Vietnamese subsets are cached when a name first needs them, by a cache-on-use branch for build files not installed. e2e: the Latin subsets are in the cache after install and the others are not.

## Refused

"Hardy to −5 °C" on labels and tiles: the figure is the habitat's coldest night at the typical cell, not a hardiness rating, and the site has said so since round four; a shorter phrase that reads as one would be the 5 % wrong the launch draft argues against. Whole degrees on labels: round fifteen made every temperature print at one precision on one page for a reason. Renaming Follow: the word says what it does. A front-page line about knowing who visits: the site already says it collects nothing, and saying it twice is what a site that collects something does.

## After the changes

`npx svelte-check --threshold warning` clean; `npx vitest run` 35 files, 333 tests; `npx playwright test tests/e2e/smoke.spec.ts --workers=1` 67 tests, no retries. For the deployer: nothing beyond `npm run deploy`, which now ends with the live check; the ceilings are defaults in code until a variable says otherwise.
